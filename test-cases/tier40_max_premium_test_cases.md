# Test Cases — Tier 40 Max Premium

## Important Spec Note

Under the **current** business rules specification, only tiers 10 (Base), 20 (Ultra), and 30 (Premium) are recognized as valid subscription tiers (BR-001). Section 12 of the spec explicitly lists "Tier 40 → Invalid" as an example invalid scenario. "Tier 40 Max Premium" does not exist as a supported tier and would require a formal amendment to BR-001 and the tier table before it can be created, upgraded to, renewed with, or used in any subscription operation.

This document is split into two groups accordingly:

- **Group 1 (TC-001 – TC-010):** Tests against the **current spec** — confirming the system correctly rejects Tier 40 today.
- **Group 2 (TC-011 – TC-025):** **Forward-looking** tests — written assuming BR-001 and the tier hierarchy table have been formally updated to add Tier 40 (Max Premium) above Premium (30), making the hierarchy **Base (10) < Ultra (20) < Premium (30) < Max Premium (40)**.
